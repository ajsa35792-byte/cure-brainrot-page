Cure Brainrot: can a school quiz interrupt doomscrolling?

FRP 2026, AI Engineering track · ISSAI, Nazarbayev University
📄 Paper ▶ Demo: download the extension 🎬 Video

In one sentence: Cure Brainrot gives short-video time back as a 10-question school quiz: 8 of 10 right answers open 20 more minutes.

The problem

Short videos eat hours without anyone noticing: we open TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels “for five minutes” and lose an evening, and then it is hard to start homework or go to sleep. It is not only about time — after a long scroll the head is tired and little was learned. Screen-time limits only stop the video; they do not give anything back.

How it works

1. Inputtime on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels
2. ModelAI-written questions at your grade, in English, Russian or Kazakh
3. Outputtabs close, a 10-question quiz, pass = 20 more minutes

The questions come from a built-in bank for grades 5, 6 and 7 in Math, Science and Social Studies, and from the class chat model, which writes 10 questions with four answers each. Without an AI key the built-in questions are used, so the quiz always works.

Results

We checked the screens with the class page-check tool: every button, the three languages, the score screen and the popup, with no red errors. For the timer we shortened the daily limit and moved a fake clock, so a whole day runs in a few seconds.

Question sourceRight answers neededExtra time
Built-in questions (no AI key)8 of 1020 minutes
AI-written questions8 of 1020 minutes

The daily limit is 20 minutes, and both question sources use the same passing rule: 8 of 10 right answers open 20 more minutes. What does not work yet: one quiz can guess your grade only roughly, so the screen says 5–6; the extension closes tabs in Chrome, but closing Instagram inside its phone app needs a real phone app; and the AI can write a wrong question.

Limitations and ethics

Our test set is small and made by us, so these numbers describe our own runs, not a whole class. We collect no private data: no camera, no microphone, no names of classmates. The AI key is saved only on the student’s computer, and the extension only closes short-video tabs — it never watches or records them.
