Cure Brainrot

A Chrome extension that gives back the evening: it closes TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels after a daily limit, and only a short school quiz unlocks 20 more minutes.

Download the extension (22.6 KB) Read the paper

The problem

Short videos eat hours without anyone noticing. Screen-time limits only stop the video; they do not give anything back. Every time you open TikTok, Shorts or Reels "for five minutes", the feed is built to keep you: endless scroll, autoplay, no stopping point.

What we built

When the daily short-video limit is used up, the extension closes those tabs and opens a 10-question quiz on your own subjects. 8 of 10 right answers give 20 extra minutes; a failed quiz keeps the sites closed for 5 minutes. A popup shows the time left and a one-line daily result you can copy.

Install (2 minutes)

  1. Download cure-brainrot-2.6.zip and unzip it — you get a folder cure-brainrot-2.6.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Click the puzzle piece → Cure Brainrot; set your limit and your name.

Works in Chrome (other Chromium browsers such as Edge work the same way).

The AI key is optional

Without a key you get built-in math and school questions, so the quiz always works. With your own key the questions are written by the AI in your chosen subjects. The key is saved only on your computer and the copied daily line never leaves it.
